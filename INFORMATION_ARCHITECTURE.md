## 02 — User Journeys & Target Audiences

### Persona A: International Enterprise Expanding to KSA
- **Entry Goal:** Establish a legal Saudi entity with foreign investment licensing (MISA).
- **Journey:** Home Hero → Establish/Activate/Operate Narrative → Business Setup Service Page → Ecosystem Technology & Banking Integration → Make The First Move Form (Enterprise Stream).
- **Key Conversion Action:** Request Market-Entry Consultation / Business Setup Discovery.

### Persona B: High-Net-Worth Individual / Foreign Investor
- **Entry Goal:** Invest in Saudi real estate or secure Saudi Premium Residency.
- **Journey:** Home Hero → Real Estate & Residency Pillars → Real Estate Service Page / Premium Residency Guide → Investment Assessment Matrix → Contact / Advisory Booking.
- **Key Conversion Action:** Request Investment Briefing / Premium Residency Evaluation.

### Persona C: International Specialist & Service Provider
- **Entry Goal:** Partner with AATIQ as a Service Partner for joint Saudi execution.
- **Journey:** Home Hero → Ecosystem Capabilities → Service Partners Page → Application Criteria → Service Partner Qualification Enquiry.
- **Key Conversion Action:** Apply to Service Partner Network.

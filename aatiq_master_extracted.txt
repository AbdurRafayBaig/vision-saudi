1. Executive Summary
ONE KINGDOM. ONE VISION.
AATIQ ALTHURAYA is being positioned as a Saudi market-entry, business, investment and real-estate platform — not simply a company-formation agent. Formation is the entry point; the differentiator is what happens afterwards.
Core journey: ESTABLISH → ACTIVATE → OPERATE → CONNECT → GROW.
Customer shorthand: ESTABLISH · OPERATE · INVEST · GROW.
Primary pillars: Business Setup, Real Estate, Premium Residency and Corporate/Business Services.
Central differentiator: clients can enter a wider Althuraya ecosystem after establishment.
Credibility: Saudi operating experience behind the team and strategic partners, with SLNEE's historical work attributed to SLNEE.
Partner layer: Service Partners for international specialists; Success Partners for eligible ecosystem businesses.
Most firms get you established. We built what comes next.
Your CR opens the door. We take you further.
2. Project Evolution
Saudi Solutions → Vision Saudi
The predecessor concept evolved from Saudi Solutions into Vision Saudi: a Saudi gateway for international investors, entrepreneurs and companies. Its services already included business setup, real estate, partner programmes, investments, technology/AI, GRO and Premium Residency.
Earlier language included “Unlocking the Kingdom of Opportunity” and “One Kingdom. Endless Possibilities.”
The model already aimed to continue beyond incorporation into operations, investment and partnerships.
Earlier 'umbrella' wording for foreign businesses is superseded and should not be reused without legal review.
Vision Saudi → Aatiq Althuraya
AATIQ ALTHURAYA is the current Saudi platform, discussed as having its own CR, banking infrastructure, domain and operating identity. It stands on its own while drawing on genuine team and partner experience.
AATIQ is new. The people, experience and network behind it aren't.

The AATIQ Service Partner Programme connects international companies and specialist providers with opportunities in Saudi Arabia. 19. Legal / Regulatory Messaging Guardrails
Communications guardrail only, not legal advice. Final public claims and contractual structures should be reviewed against current Saudi law and the company's actual licences.
Do not say foreign businesses can simply operate 'under our umbrella' or use AATIQ's CR as their own.
Do not promise blanket 'full revenue rights'.
Use genuine service, subcontracting, technology, consultancy or project-delivery agreements where appropriate.
State that Service Partner engagements are structured according to the project, activities and applicable Saudi requirements.
Do not imply guaranteed access to government projects/tenders.
Do not call SLNEE a parent or AATIQ a subsidiary unless legally true.
Attribute historical SLNEE work to SLNEE.
Use government/client logos only with a defensible basis and permissions where required.
Do not guarantee property yields or investment returns.
Verify Premium Residency and real-estate rules before publication.
Verify REGA and other licence/approval wording against the exact licence held.

## 02 — DETAILED ROUTE INSPECTION & REFINEMENT SPECIFICATIONS

### 1. `/` — HOMEPAGE
- **URL:** `/`
- **Purpose:** Primary digital flag, brand anchor, narrative journey, ecosystem introduction.
- **Target Audience:** International enterprise leaders, HNW investors, regional founders, strategic partners.
- **Primary CTA:** "Make the First Move" (Triggers Modal)
- **Secondary CTA:** "Explore AATIQ" (`#opportunity`)
- **Hero:** Full-bleed cinematic image of Riyadh skyline (`/images/hero-riyadh.png`), dark gradient overlay, Playfair Display typography.
- **Content Sections:** Hero → The Opportunity → Brand Statement → 4-Direction Service Navigator → 5-Stage Journey Narrative → Ecosystem Explorer → Real Estate Highlight → Experience Showcase → Why AATIQ → Final CTA.
- **Visual Status:** Benchmark Quality (10/10).
- **Issues:** Logo link in Navbar needs verification for internal route transitions; header text contrast needs automatic adjustment on non-dark heroes.

### 2. `/services` — SERVICES INDEX
- **URL:** `/services`
- **Purpose:** Full spectrum showcase of public pillars and ecosystem capabilities.
- **Target Audience:** Prospective clients looking to compare services or explore total ecosystem scope.
- **Primary CTA:** "Start Company Establishment" / "Make the First Move"
- **Secondary CTA:** Individual pillar deep-dive links (`/services/business-setup`, etc.)
- **Hero Type:** **Hero Type B (Editorial Split)** — High-contrast headline on left, key pillars preview image/media on right.
- **Content Sections:** Hero → Service Spectrum Accordion/Spotlight → Pillar Deep Dives → Ecosystem Integration Matrix → Next Steps CTA.
- **Issues to Fix:** Currently using old `#070A0E` dark theme with neon `#22BD98` green text and monospace tags. Needs conversion to `--cream` / `--charcoal` design system with `Playfair Display` headlines and `--saudi-green` accents.
